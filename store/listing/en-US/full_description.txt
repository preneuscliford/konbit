Konbit 509 brings the spirit of the Haitian konbit to your phone: "Men anpil, chay pa lou" — many hands make the load light. Organize actions together in your neighborhood, share your ideas and put the businesses of your town on the map, in Haiti and across the diaspora.

🤝 LAUNCH A KONBIT
A street clean-up, a community garden, a fundraiser, a local project: give it a name, a town, a date and a duration, and set a number of spots if you like. Anyone can see and share it; people who say "Mwen ladan" (I'm in) join the group and talk together inside the konbit.

💬 POST AND CONNECT
Write a post, add photos and emojis, reply, like, repost or quote ("Site") a post with your own message. Follow the people who inspire you and get notified when they post.

🏪 BIZNIS: YOUR BUSINESS PAGE AND CATALOG
Entrepreneurs, create a free page for your business: description, address, opening hours, and direct contact through WhatsApp or phone. Add your products and services to your katalòg: every item gets its own page with photos, price and a link to share. You can see how many people viewed each item.

📈 ENFO JODI A (TODAY'S INFO)
Every day, the dollar rate (real market and BRH) with its history, an HTG / USD / EUR / CAD converter, and the weather in your town.

🌍 IN KREYÒL AND FRENCH
The app speaks Haitian Creole and French, in light, dark or automatic mode. Everything is also available on konbit509.app, from any computer.

🛡️ A RESPECTFUL COMMUNITY
Clear rules, reporting of abusive content and a moderation team. You can delete your account and your data at any time, from the app or the website.

Konbit 509 is in testing: your feedback helps us improve it. Write to us at konbit@haitiscope.com.

Ansanm, nou wè pi lwen.
